## Description
PigeonX helps restaurants, hotels, rooftops and patios keep pigeons and other birds away without spikes, nets, traps or chemicals.

Pick a sound. Press Start. Birds leave.

What you get
- Nine built-in sounds, from steady high pitch to bird alarm calls
- Plays on your phone, a Bluetooth speaker, or a PigeonX speaker
- A clear answer on whether your speaker can play each sound
- A mark on sounds some people can hear
- Schedules around your service hours
- A history of what played and when

Pro adds every sound, a sound builder, schedules and no time limit.
Business adds places, areas, speakers, teammates and a web dashboard at pigeonx.org.

PigeonX is honest about what works. Phone speakers play up to about 18 kHz. Bluetooth speakers about 19 kHz. PigeonX speakers reach 25 kHz. Sounds above 17 kHz may be audible to some guests, and we say so in the app.

## Review notes
The app plays generated audio through the device speaker or connected Bluetooth audio. It does not record audio; the microphone usage string exists only because the audio library links microphone APIs. Sign in with Apple and email sign-in are optional; the app works as a guest. No account is needed to test. Background audio keeps a started sound playing when the screen locks.
