# PigeonX brand brief (owner's memo, 2026-08-21)

Smarter, humane bird deterrence for the places people gather.

PigeonX is a mobile-first bird deterrence platform designed to help restaurants, hotels, rooftops, patios, and other commercial properties manage nuisance pigeons and birds without relying on chemicals, traps, spikes, or visually intrusive systems.

## The Problem

Pigeons and nuisance birds create persistent problems for hospitality and commercial properties:

- Outdoor dining disruption and poor guest experiences
- Droppings on tables, furniture, balconies, rooftops, and walkways
- Recurring cleaning and maintenance costs
- Food-safety and sanitation concerns
- Existing deterrents can be expensive, unsightly, labor-intensive, or inconsistent

For many businesses, bird control remains an analog problem without a simple, technology-driven solution.

## The PigeonX Solution

PigeonX turns a smartphone into the control center for a smarter bird-deterrence system.

The iOS and Android app can generate configurable high-frequency audio and route it through compatible external speakers or dedicated PigeonX hardware, allowing commercial users to manage deterrence across outdoor spaces.

## Core Platform

- Frequency Control: configure and test different audio profiles
- Bluetooth Connectivity: connect to compatible external speakers and future PigeonX hardware
- Scheduling: automate operation around dining and business hours
- Zone Management: manage multiple speakers and locations
- Commercial Dashboard: centralized controls for restaurants, hotels, property managers, and multi-location operators
- Future Smart Detection: motion/camera-assisted activation and automated deterrence profiles

## Business Model

- PigeonX Free: entry-level app functionality designed to drive adoption and product awareness.
- PigeonX Pro: premium features, advanced controls, scheduling, saved profiles, and enhanced Bluetooth functionality.
- PigeonX Business: recurring subscription for restaurants, hotels, rooftops, resorts, property managers, warehouses, and other commercial users.
- PigeonX Hardware: purpose-built connected emitters/speakers sold or leased alongside the software platform.
- PigeonX Enterprise: multi-location management, analytics, remote monitoring, centralized controls, and enterprise support.

## Go-to-Market

PigeonX can initially target restaurants and hotels with outdoor spaces, where the problem is visible, frequent, and directly impacts the customer experience.

Early pilots can establish measurable proof points around bird activity, cleaning costs, customer complaints, and operational effectiveness. Successful locations become reference customers for expansion into restaurant groups, hotel chains, resorts, property managers, municipalities, transportation facilities, warehouses, and other commercial environments.

## The Bigger Opportunity

The long-term opportunity isn't simply a pigeon-repelling app.

PigeonX can become a connected smart pest-deterrence platform, combining software, sensors, audio hardware, automation, and data to help commercial properties manage unwanted wildlife.

The platform can ultimately expand beyond pigeons into additional bird species and other addressable nuisance-pest categories.

## Why PigeonX Can Win

Simple. Humane. Connected. Scalable.

Instead of selling another standalone pest-control device, PigeonX is building a software-controlled ecosystem that can improve over time, support multiple hardware configurations, and generate recurring revenue from commercial customers.

PigeonX.org: turning bird control from an analog nuisance into a connected technology platform.
